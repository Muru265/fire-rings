## Gate history

**v1.1 (Round 5B, 12 Nov 2026)** — V3 passes if the harmonized 2012 step is consistent with the MODIS-only step AND the
harmonized annual series (2003–2019) has no Pettitt change point (p > 0.05).

**v1.2 (adopted 13 Nov 2026)** — V3 passes if the step is consistent (unchanged) AND either (a) no harmonized Pettitt
change point (p > 0.05), or (b) the change year lies outside 2011–2013 (not at the MODIS→VIIRS seam) and the MODIS-only
series has a significant change point (p ≤ 0.05) in the same year ±1 (`lib.calib.v3_gate`).

*Why:* v1.1 rejected any break, including real fire history that MODIS alone also records long before VIIRS existed.
V3 exists to catch the seam, so v1.2 restricts it to that. The rule was fixed first and checked read-only on all 9
regions before adoption: no published region changes; Sudan and Mato Grosso flip fail → pass; an exact-year window
(±0) gives the same result as ±1. *Caveat:* before 2012 the harmonized series IS MODIS (and Pettitt is rank-based), so
branch (b) is not an independent test of pre-2012 breaks; the seam is guarded by the step test plus "no break at
2011–2013".
