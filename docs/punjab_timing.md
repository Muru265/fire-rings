## Conclusion

**The data support a shift of Punjab's Oct–Nov burning to late afternoon.** Relative to 2013–2019, the daytime Aqua÷S-NPP ratio in Punjab Oct–Nov rose year by year: 2022 ×1.054, 2023 ×1.127, 2024 ×1.279, 2025 ×1.832, 2026 ×2.015. Against NOAA-20 (independent VIIRS at the same ~13:30 time) the rise is the same (2025 ×1.719, 2026 ×1.809). Over the same years the ratio stays flat or falls elsewhere: Punjab Apr–May ×0.953, Madagascar ×1.021, SE Australia ×0.862. Aqua's overpass has drifted later since Dec 2021, so the signal says that more of Punjab's autumn fires are now burning after the VIIRS overpass but before Aqua's later one. Consistent with this, morning Terra sees far fewer (×0.354) and the share of VIIRS night detections (~01:30) rose ×6.212.

A change in fire *size* would push the ratio the other way (coarser MODIS misses small fires), so it does not explain the rise.

**Caveats.** The 2025 and 2026 ratios rest on few fires (S-NPP day cell-days 4,257 and 1,138); 2026 is near-real-time and compared only up to S-NPP's last day (1 Nov). We cannot measure how much burning happens between overpasses, so we do not correct the numbers.

**What Fire Rings does.** Harmonized values are unchanged (documented limitation, not a correction). Punjab Oct–Nov months from 2022 carry the caveat: *“Detected at satellite overpass times; burning has reportedly shifted to late afternoon, so recent seasons may be undercounted.”* This shows on headlines, the month drawer, the Proof page and in the AI tools.
